## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Information Asset Register is to identify and document the information assets associated with the simulated Software Engineering & Application Development environment.

It helps identify asset ownership, location, classification, and information security importance.

## 3. Information Classification

The following classification levels are assumed for this simulation:

- **Public:** Information approved for public distribution.
- **Internal:** Information intended for authorized internal use.
- **Confidential:** Sensitive business or client information requiring restricted access.
- **Restricted:** Highly sensitive information requiring strict access control.

## 4. Asset Ownership Responsibilities

Asset Owners are responsible for:

- Ensuring appropriate access to their assets.
- Identifying information security risks.
- Reviewing asset classification.
- Ensuring appropriate protection requirements.
- Reporting security incidents involving their assets.

## 5. Notes and Assumptions

- All listed assets are fictional examples.
- Asset owners and storage locations are simulated.
- Actual company asset inventories were not accessed.
- Classification levels are proposed assumptions and require validation and approval.
- This register does not represent the actual asset inventory of Riseup Labs.
