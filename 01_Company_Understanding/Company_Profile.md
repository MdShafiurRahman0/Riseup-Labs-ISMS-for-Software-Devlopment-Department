## 1. Company Overview

Riseup Labs is a Bangladesh-headquartered global technology company established in 2009. It provides software engineering, AI and automation, cloud infrastructure, business process outsourcing (BPO), and customer experience solutions.

The company serves global enterprises, development agencies, and international organizations.

## 2. Business Activities

- Software Engineering and Application Development
- AI, Automation and Data Solutions
- Cloud Infrastructure and Managed Services
- Business Process Outsourcing
- Customer Experience Solutions
- Staff Augmentation
- IT Consulting and Technology Advisory

## 3. Products and Services

Riseup Labs provides technology and digital transformation services to organizations across multiple industries.

Its services include custom software development, AI-powered solutions, application operations, cloud operations, managed security operations, and customer experience solutions.
